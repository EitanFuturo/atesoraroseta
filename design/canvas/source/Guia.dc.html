How to build this site

Everything a developer, or a Claude Code agent, needs to turn these artboards into the real site. Layout values are inline in each artboard. Tokens, shared text styles, hover and focus states and the responsive overrides live in site.css, the stylesheet every page links, ready to reuse.

Artwork tile

Each work is contained in a 4:5 cell at its own proportions and centred, like works hung on a common line. The whole tile is one link to its page.

[Título] [Año] · Carteles arrancados
Rest
drop-shadow 0 2 3 / 30% + 0 18 30 / 42%, black
Hover and keyboard focus
Lifts 6px; shadow deepens to 0 4 6 / 28% + 0 34 46 / 56%; 700ms
Label
Smoked glass: veil, blur 14, inner hairline at 8%. Inset 14, padding 11/14/12, radius 2. Fades in (350ms) and rises 8px (450ms) after 60ms
Why drop-shadow
A filter shadow follows the alpha, so a cut-out of the torn paper casts a torn shadow; a plain photo still gets a soft rectangle
Touch and reduced motion
No hover on touch: no label, a tap opens the page. With reduced motion, nothing moves; opacity only

States and motion

One easing everywhere, slow enough to feel like paper being lifted, never bouncy.

Easing
cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)
Durations
Tile lift and shadow 700ms · label 350/450ms · photo tile 600ms · link colour 250ms
Menu

Text links
Ink with a 1px underline at 30%; hover turns ochre
Focus
2px ochre outline, offset 4; on tiles it rings the artwork at offset 10 and shows the label

Artworks: 2400px on the long edge, WebP or AVIF. Best cut out along the torn edge on a transparent background so the shadow follows the paper, as the sample piece is here; otherwise crop to the paper's edge. Detail and street photos: 2000px, any ratio. Always set width and height, and lazy-load below the first row.

Placeholders in this canvas

Obra tile 01 and the artwork page use the sample piece (screenshot avatar retouched out, black background cut away); the detail photos are close-ups of it. Tiles 02–09 are crops of the same piece standing in for the rest of the series. Torn Posters tiles and the portrait are flat placeholders. Bracketed text, such as [Título de la obra], [Año], [soporte] and [tu-correo@dominio.com], is content still to be written. The Font options artboard records the type exploration; D · Fashion was chosen.
